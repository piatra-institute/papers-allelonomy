# Brief

Written before research begins.

## Question

Modernity's emancipation from guardianship produced a figure it did not intend: the sovereign chooser, whose capacities, preferences, and menu of options were formed by institutions and technical systems that have disappeared from the chooser's self-description, and who therefore experiences produced choices as original self-authorship, epistemic dependence as humiliation, and collective obligation as intrusion. If the ward and the sovereign are both wrong about what a person is, what is the third position, and can it be given a name, a definition, and a measurable content?

## Claim

The third position is **allelonomy**: from allelon (one another) and nomos (law), self-rule through one another. Where heteronomy is rule by another and sovereign autonomy claims rule without others, allelonomy is the capacity of distinct persons to govern themselves through reciprocal, contestable, and non-dominating relations that also participate in forming them. Four moves carry the paper:

1. **The genealogy of the mutation.** The Enlightenment's exit from self-incurred tutelage cultivated judgment, and judgment was never solitary: reasoning for oneself does not mean reasoning alone. The mutation runs from the right of self-government to the claim of final jurisdiction to the claim of exemption from shared standards, and the sovereignty it borrows is the political theology of final authority applied to the self. The produced sovereign is the figure at the end of the mutation, and the production is documented: subjectivation, habitus, technical memory, modulation, and a capitalism that absorbed the critique of authority and returned it as the obligation of self-management.
2. **The recursive loop.** Choice is neither the origin nor the end of autonomy; it is a moment inside a loop that produces choosers and is reproduced by their choices: conditions of formation, capacity for judgment, choice, aggregate sorting, new conditions of formation. Sovereignty discourse erases the first and last arrows. The loop is simulated: mild private preferences plus formation feedback stratify capacities across generations with no planner and no villain, exit by the resourced drains the voice that maintains institutions, and the decay spiral wears the costume of freedom.
3. **Allelonomy defined, with its own pathology priced.** The term's genealogy runs through Naniwada's use for mutual dependence, and mutual dependence alone is nothing: a cartel, a caste, and a conspiracy network are all mutually dependent. Four conditions make dependence emancipatory: distinction (persons remain independent centers of judgment), contestability (forming relations open to challenge and revision), non-domination (no uncontrolled power over the conditions of one's agency), recursive responsibility (answerable for what aggregated choices reproduce). The simulation prices the conditions separately: dependence without distinction herds and collapses; deference without contestability tracks a corrupted institution all the way down; the allelonomic configuration alone stays accurate under both honest and corrupted institutions.
4. **The domains.** Medicine (expertise without guardianship, agency without epistemic sovereignty: the logic of care against the logic of choice), education (the school choice loop), knowledge (the sovereign researcher's invisible infrastructure; critique against universalized suspicion), and the resulting institutional demand: trustworthy, transparent, contestable, corrigible epistemic institutions rather than commanded trust.

## Kind

**formal-model** wrapped around a synthesis — ships a simulation. `has_simulation: true`, `claims_target: results.json`.

Three analyses: the recursive sorting loop (stratification of capacities across generations under sovereign choice; a recursive-responsibility term reducing it at measured private cost); exit and voice (institutional quality dynamics under exit-only versus voice-bearing regimes; the spiral in which privileged exit removes the articulate critics); and epistemic regimes (ward, sovereign, allelonomic, and herding agents against honest and corrupted expert institutions; accuracy grid). Results the model must produce or refute: stratification without a planner; the decay spiral; ward accuracy collapsing with institutional corruption while allelonomic accuracy survives it; the herding cell losing to the sovereign cell somewhere (interdependence alone is not emancipatory); and the private cost of responsibility stated rather than hidden.

## Constraint

The paper stands alone, cites no PIATRA paper, and does not reuse the seed's prose. Sloterdijk is one bounded moment (anthropotechnics), never the frame; Schmitt and Deleuze are instruments with stop conditions; Friston is at most one sentence. The paper does not run the eugenics extension; the school case is distributed developmental selection and stops there.
